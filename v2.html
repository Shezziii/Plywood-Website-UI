About Us

Built on Quality,
Designed for Your World

Woodborn Ply has built a profound legacy within the timber industry, establishing unwavering trust through consistent strength and quality. We specialise in transforming raw wood into exclusive, high-calibre solutions, empowering visionary architects, pioneering designers, and corporate partners.

Located in Ganemulla, Sri Lanka, we sell plywood boards and sheets in any quantity — from single boards for home projects to bulk orders for large commercial builds. Every product we offer is engineered for unyielding protection, enduring strength, and true eco-harmony.

What We Offer

Our Products

Plywood & Block Board

Woodborn Plywood presents a wide range of product choices for the discerning customer. Our plywood range is made with the finest quality timber and produced with precision engineering backed by eco-conscious manufacturing processes. Available in multiple grades and thicknesses.

View Product Details →

Doors

Made with premium BWP Grade phenol formaldehyde resin for superior durability. 100% calibrated for uniform thickness, ensuring better load distribution. Resilient to temperature fluctuations, moisture, and daily wear. Available in flush and panel designs.

View Product Details →

Prelam Plywood

Woodborn Prelam Plywood offers a diverse range of pre-laminated plywood designs, ensuring aesthetic appeal and functionality for various interior and architectural applications. Our collection features beautiful textures, patterns and finishes to suit any décor.

View Product Details →

MDF Boards

High Density Xtreme Moisture Resistant (HDXMR). When moisture strikes and pressure builds — Woodborn MDF endures. Built with high-density bonding and advanced moisture-proof technology, it's your ultimate performance partner for kitchens, bathrooms and high-humidity spaces.

View Product Details →

WPC Boards

WPC boards are a blend of wood fibre and thermoplastics, offering the best of both worlds — the look of wood with the durability of plastic. Termite-proof, water-resistant, and extremely low maintenance. Also available in door and frame formats.

View Product Details →
How We Make It

Technology & R&D

1

Handpicking Suitable Timber

Only the finest grade raw timber is selected for processing.

2

Precision Peeling of Core Veneer

Uniform veneer sheets are peeled with precision machinery.

3

Controlled Drying

Moisture content is reduced to optimal levels in controlled chambers.

4

Automatic Core Composing

Veneers are assembled automatically for zero-gap bonding.

5

Advanced Resin Formation

High-grade resins are applied for waterproof, long-lasting bonds.

6

Hot & Cold Press

Dual-press process ensures maximum strength and uniform thickness.
