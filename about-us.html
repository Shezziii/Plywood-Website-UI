Our Story

From a Small Plank Shop to a Full Plywood Supplier

Sandakelum was founded with a simple mission: offer quality wood materials at honest prices to the people of Ganemulla and the surrounding areas. What began as a modest shop selling raw timber planks has grown steadily over two decades.

Today we stock imported shutter boards, hard boards, MDF melamine, PVC sheets, eco sheets, and cladding sheets — covering every need from residential renovations to large commercial builds. Our warehouse is open 7 days a week.

We are proud to have served over 500 satisfied customers across Sri Lanka, and we remain committed to the same values that started it all: quality materials, fair pricing, and honest advice.
